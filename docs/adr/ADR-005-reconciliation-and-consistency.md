# ADR-005 — Reconciliation and consistency model

**Status:** Proposed<br>
**Decision owner:** Project maintainer<br>
**Review gate:** Must be Accepted before production coding

## Context

Jellyfin events may be broad, duplicated, generated by the plugin’s own writes, or emitted in large bursts. Multi-operation plans are not transactional.

## Decision

1. Events mark eligible items dirty; event handlers do not directly mutate Jellyfin state.
2. A deduplicating in-memory dirty set coalesces repeated events.
3. One serialized coordinator owns all synchronization mutations.
4. The planner computes complete desired state; it does not blindly translate events into opposite actions.
5. Self-generated events are tolerated through idempotence.
6. Normal activity is near-real-time.
7. Large scans/event storms degrade to broader Full Reconcile rather than unbounded queue growth.
8. The consistency model is eventual.
9. On item mutation failure:
   - keep partial state;
   - stop remaining operations for that item;
   - do not rollback;
   - defer retry until Full Reconcile.
10. Full Reconcile continues after individual item failures and reports a summary.

## Consequences

- Runtime recursion does not become an infinite write loop.
- Missed events and partial writes are repairable.
- A failed item may remain temporarily inconsistent.
- Full Reconcile is a required operational feature, not an optional optimization.
- No transaction or rollback framework is required.

## Required examples

```text
Plugin adds Animation membership.
Jellyfin emits collection-added event.
Item queues again.
Second plan has zero delta.
```

```text
Operation 1 succeeds.
Operation 2 fails.
Operation 1 remains.
Item waits for Full Reconcile.
```
